# Aether Library

A personal AI library that grows with you.

A multi-AI workspace where different AI perspectives collaborate, while your knowledge stays on your device.

## Features

- 🏛️ AI Council — bring multiple AI perspectives together
- 📚 Vault — preserve your conversations and insights locally
- 🔒 Local-first — your knowledge stays private
- 🌐 Multi-provider support — use your own AI providers
